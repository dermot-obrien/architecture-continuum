# Exemplar: the ServiceNow knowledge agent in Teams

A worked example of the [Architecture Assessment Standard](../../standard-architecture-assessment.md), kept in this one folder so that it can be deleted as a whole. Nothing outside this folder links to it or depends on it: delete `standards/assessment/examples/servicenow-knowledge-agent/` and run `node scripts/sync-skill-standards.mjs` to remove the skill's copy. The design, its catalogue identifiers and its target-state positions are illustrative.

The records keep the paths and lens versions they were made with: they name `standards/assessment/examples/SD-002-servicenow-knowledge-agent/` and the flat files one level up, from before the exemplar was gathered into this folder, and AS-005 was assessed against core lens 0.4.0.

## How the runs went

The method was tested end to end on a real design: a Copilot Studio agent in Teams that answers staff questions from ServiceNow knowledge articles indexed by an existing Copilot connector connection. The design was written as a specification, [SD-002](earlier-runs/SD-002-servicenow-knowledge-agent.design.yaml), with its author's triage, [TR-001](earlier-runs/TR-001-servicenow-knowledge-agent.triage.yaml). A separate agent, given only the `architecture` skill's triage and assess flows and the files, re-derived the triage (AT2, as declared, with an unsourced novelty answer flagged), applied all 77 criteria of the core and AI solution lenses, and proposed endorsed with conditions: no blocker, twelve majors in three risk themes. Its record is [AS-002](earlier-runs/AS-002-servicenow-knowledge-agent.assessment.yaml) and its [report](earlier-runs/AS-002-servicenow-knowledge-agent.report.md) gives the triage, the path taken and the findings.

The test also found eleven faults in the method, all since fixed: a gap in the outcome rules (now applied in order, with a design fix that has no due date counting against endorsement); no line between missing evidence that defers an assessment and missing evidence that is a finding; one result field for the two halves of a `both` criterion (now `tool_result` beside `result`); ambiguous rules on which connectors cross a bounded context, which way untrusted content flows, which scenarios count for a quality and what counts as a cost model; rules a managed platform such as Copilot Studio cannot meet as written (pinning, call limits, telemetry conventions, vendor contracts), which now accept a recorded decision instead; a triage plan that listed the production lens for a design review; novelty that could not be derived from a specification (now `assessed_by` on the platform's element); a sample-size rule that did not say how many negatives a false-answer ceiling needs; and an independence rule that let an agent stand alone as the independent assessor at AT2. The record and report keep the lens versions they were made against, core 0.2.0 and AI solution 0.2.0.

A second run tested the fixed method end to end on the same design version, with one new input: the responsible architect's attestation that the ServiceNow connector and the Microsoft 365 ingestion and semantic querying building blocks are not target state, while Copilot Studio, Teams and Entra ID are. A fresh agent re-triaged the design ([TR-002](earlier-runs/TR-002-servicenow-knowledge-agent.triage.yaml), AT3, because novelty read the blocks' missing catalogue references as new to the landscape), assessed it against core 0.3.0 and AI solution 0.3.0 ([AS-003](earlier-runs/AS-003-servicenow-knowledge-agent.assessment.yaml)), and wrote the [report](earlier-runs/AS-003-servicenow-knowledge-agent.report.md) and a slide [readout](earlier-runs/AS-003-servicenow-knowledge-agent.readout.md) with catalogue identifiers linked. The target-state criteria now found the departures the attestation implies: grounding rests on blocks that are not target, with no departure recorded and no successor named. The proposed outcome is endorsed with conditions: no blocker open, 22 majors in four risk themes. The run left nine questions for the next version of the method, among them that an attested position should win over the missing-reference rule for novelty, that onboarding a new component should say whether it stops an assessment, that `CORE-STR-05` should resolve references to decisions and evals, and that an assessor's proposed due dates should not decide the outcome on their own.

A third run followed the responsible architect's correction of the target state. The Microsoft Graph index is target, but only for content that starts in Microsoft 365; copying content from outside Microsoft into it is not target; and knowledge from outside Microsoft belongs in a separate enterprise knowledge store using vector and graph retrieval. That took two additions to the method: a `not-target` position for technology that is neither in use nor planned, and a blocker, `CORE-TGT-05`, met only by an exception approved through escalation to the exception authority, never by the responsible architect alone. [TR-004](earlier-runs/TR-004-servicenow-knowledge-agent.triage.yaml) and [AS-004](earlier-runs/AS-004-servicenow-knowledge-agent.assessment.yaml) record the result: not endorsed, one blocker and 19 important gaps, with a plain-language [report](earlier-runs/AS-004-servicenow-knowledge-agent.report.md) and [readout](earlier-runs/AS-004-servicenow-knowledge-agent.readout.md) that name the way back, grounding the assistant on the enterprise knowledge store. The earlier records stay as they were made, against the inputs of their time.

A fourth run started where the method intends: from a design document. The design was written as a solution design from the pattern skill's solution design template, whose tables carry the fields a specification needs, with its component diagram and scenario walkthroughs generated from those tables. The `specify` flow translated it into a specification and 49 questions for the author, and an independent agent ran `triage`, `assess` and `report` on that. The whole chain is in [one folder](end-to-end/README.md). The outcome is the same, not endorsed on the one blocker, with 22 important gaps rather than 19: the fuller document closed none of AS-004's shortfalls, but it stated the indexing path, the connector's sign-in and the identity mapping, and that exposed new ones, such as an interface to Entra ID with no authentication recorded. That run also showed the position vocabulary was short of one case: the connection was in use but not to be extended, which is neither `baseline` (a successor to come) nor `not-target` (not in use). Triage 0.3.0 and core 0.5.0 add `contained` for it: existing uses carry on, a new use is a departure at AT2 and a blocker under `CORE-TGT-05` until an exception is approved through escalation.

## Architecture and cybersecurity, in this case

The ServiceNow knowledge agent in Teams ([SD-002](earlier-runs/SD-002-servicenow-knowledge-agent.design.yaml)) shows the split in practice. Its assessment, [AS-003](earlier-runs/AS-003-servicenow-knowledge-agent.report.md), found on the architecture side that the design has no threat model, does not mark every path that carries untrusted content, does not state the agent's identity or the breadth of its delegated scope, and plans no injection or red-team eval. Those are design findings, closed by changing the design. Whether the connection really carries ServiceNow's permissions into the index, whether the delegated scope is granted as designed, and whether leakage probes pass in the tenant are handed over to the cybersecurity assessment, whose outcome must return before production entry.
